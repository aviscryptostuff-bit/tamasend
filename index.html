On the Tamagotchi

  1. Make sure it's awake. It refuses connections while sleeping; setting its clock to daytime wakes it.
  2. Go to Connection → APP, back out, then Connection → Download.
  3. While it's on the waiting screen, tap Connect & send and pick TMGC_meets.
