Marry your Tama

Your Tama has to be an adult. MyMeets' marriage works in two connections: first your Tama visits, then it comes back married.

STEP 1 · VISIT
  1. On the Tama: Connection (ツーしん) → App (アプリ) → Visit (あそびにいく), the top option.
  2. While it waits, tap the button and pick TMGC_meets.
STEP 2 · MARRY
  1. Press any button on the Tama.
  2. Go to Connection → App again. When it asks Return? (かえる？), pick Yes (はい).
  3. While it waits, tap Marry and pick TMGC_meets.
Use "bring back" if your Tama isn't an adult yet, or if you change your mind. It follows the same steps as Marry.
MyMeets refuses this, and nobody knows what the Tama does with it. It might ignore it, or it might mess up your Tama's save. Only try it if you're OK with that.
