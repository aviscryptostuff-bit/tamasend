Magic Hut and Moon Terrace for Tamagotchi ON / Meets

Download the two text files: magic_hut.txt and moon_terrace.txt

Load a text file on WebMeets "Compiled files" webpage: https://tamagotchicenter.com/webmeets/custom-items?itemType=compiled%20files
On the Tamagotchi, go to the connection menu, select App -> Download

It will download a new location on your Tamagotchi.
